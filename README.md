### Multi-Agent GTM Platform

- **Problem:** Growth teams ran research, outreach, content and CRM updates across a dozen disconnected tools.
- **Built:** An agent platform with 130+ specialised agents behind a drag-and-drop workflow builder, multi-LLM routing across Claude, OpenAI and Gemini, MCP tool servers, browser agents for research, and per-tenant knowledge bases.
- **Scale:** Multi-tenant, queue-backed execution with Socket.IO streaming of every agent step to the UI.

`Next.js` `Node.js` `MongoDB` `Redis` `BullMQ` `LangGraph` `MCP` `Playwright`

### Healthcare Voice AI

- **Problem:** A clinic network could not call every patient about medication adherence by hand.
- **Built:** An outbound voice system that runs the calls, handles follow-up questions, escalates to staff when needed and writes outcomes back to the record. PHI never reaches a general-purpose model unmasked.
- **Scale:** 200+ patient calls a night, HIPAA-aware end to end.

`Vapi` `Twilio` `n8n` `GPT` `Node.js` `PostgreSQL`

### Document Automation

- **Problem:** Invoices arrived in every format, and a finance team keyed them in by hand.
- **Built:** An extraction pipeline with LLM schema mapping, validation rules and a human review queue for the edge cases.
- **Result:** 90% of the manual work removed. A second pass on model routing and caching then cut AI processing cost by 50%.

`Python` `FastAPI` `Azure OpenAI` `PostgreSQL`

### Sales AI for HubSpot

- **Problem:** Inbound leads waited hours for a first reply and meetings were booked by hand.
- **Built:** A HubSpot-integrated assistant that qualifies leads from the conversation, updates the CRM and books the meeting directly.
- **Result:** 60% faster response to new leads.

`Node.js` `HubSpot API` `GPT` `Nylas`

### Legal Research AI

- **Problem:** Associates spent half their time assembling case briefs.
- **Built:** A RAG system over case law that drafts briefs with citations and keeps an audit trail of every source used.
- **Result:** 50% research time saved, with every claim traceable.

`Python` `LangChain` `Pinecone` `Cohere Rerank` `Next.js`

### CRM SaaS at Scale

- **Problem:** A CRM product growing past the point where a hand-built backend could keep up.
- **Built:** Multi-tenant architecture, role-based access, billing and real-time dashboards on React and Node, delivered for enterprise clients.
- **Scale:** 40,000+ users.

`React` `Node.js` `Socket.IO` `PostgreSQL` `Redis` `AWS`
